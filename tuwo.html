Tuwo Shinkafa

Medium 30 mins 4 Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Soft Rice (Shinkafa)
  • 4-5 cups Water
  • Pinch of Salt (optional)

Cultural Significance

Tuwo Shinkafa is a staple in Northern Nigeria, usually served with Miyan Kuka or Miyan Taushe during special occasions and Friday lunches.

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Rice: Wash the rice thoroughly with cold water to remove excess starch.
  2. Boil: Place the rice in a large pot with 3 cups of water. Cook on medium heat until the rice is very soft and the water is almost absorbed.
  3. Mash: Using a wooden spatula (muciya), mash the rice against the sides of the pot until it becomes a smooth, dough-like consistency.
  4. Steam: Add a little more water if it's too thick, cover tightly, and let it steam on low heat for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve: Mold into balls using a wet bowl or wrap in transparent nylon to keep it moist. Serve hot with your favorite Hausa soup.
